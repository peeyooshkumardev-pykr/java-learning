### Q01 — Find the Maximum Sum Subarray

Take an array and find the contiguous subarray having the maximum sum.

**Example:**

```text
Enter N = 8
Enter elements:
-2 1 -3 4 -1 2 1 -5

Maximum sum subarray:
4 -1 2 1

Maximum sum = 6
```
